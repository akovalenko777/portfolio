nofagroup.com.ua

A closed-loop system for the accounting of sunflower and rapeseed oil, record-keeping and the calculation of analyses.

Full development cycle: from requirements analysis, planning and database structure design to a fully finished product.

Generation of reports and other documents for printing and export to Excel ‘on the fly’.

The interface is implemented as a single-page application (SPA) using AJAX without any additional libraries.
