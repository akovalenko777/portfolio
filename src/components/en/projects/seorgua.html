se.org.ua

A platform run by a civil society organisation that implements digital accessibility solutions for deaf people in Ukraine: a map of accessible facilities, a catalogue of projects, and contact forms.

The website was built using a custom content management system.

Thanks to specially designed logic and the caching of static files, the website loads quickly.
