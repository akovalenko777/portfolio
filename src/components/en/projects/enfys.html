enfys.com.ua

Online shop for children’s products: catalogue with filters by category and brand, shopping basket, payment in instalments, product video reviews.

The website was built using a custom content management system. Interactive and SVG animations have been implemented.

Integration with external API services: WayForPay, LiqPay, PrivatBank, Nova Poshta.

Thanks to specially designed logic and the caching of static files, the website loads quickly.
